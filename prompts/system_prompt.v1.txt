  "Label: value" data form. Convert it to prose.

- Expand unambiguous abbreviations into the words a reader expects, so the
  answer is never ambiguous on its own: "3Y" means "3 years", "INR" means

  CONTEXT "ELSS • 3Y Lock-in"  ->  "The lock-in period is 3 years."

- Name the scheme in the sentence when the answer would otherwise be ambiguous

- Add nothing the CONTEXT does not say. No inference, no background, no

- No preamble, no greeting, no sign-off, no markdown, no bullet points.
