> **Facts-only. No investment advice.**

Every answer below was produced by running the real engine in `src/retrieval_engine.py`
against the committed Chroma index, not written by hand. Each records the answer the
app returned, the citation it resolved from the retrieved chunk, and which generator
produced it. Regenerate with the driver noted in the README.

Six are answerable facts, one per scheme. Six must be declined: one off-corpus
process question, three advice-shaped, and two carrying personal information. The
refusals are the harder half of the contract — a pipeline that answers everything is
worse than one that answers nothing.

## How this was generated

Run against the committed index via `E.answer_with_trace()`. Of 12 questions:

- **6** — not called (refused before generation)
- **5** — `llm` (Groq returned `ok`)
- **1** — `extractive` (the `llm` path was tried and Groq refused)

> This run was **mixed**. The Groq daily token quota was exhausted partway
> through, so question 6 (riskometer) fell back to the extractive path. The
> answer text is correct and cited either way, and the `Generated by` row on
> each question says which path produced it. A run with quota available
> answers all six on the `llm` path.

## How to read these

**The citation is resolved from the retrieved chunk, not from the generated text.**
That is what guarantees the link cannot drift to a different page than the evidence
the answer was built from — including when the answer is truncated at the
sentence cap.

**Sentences are counted, not estimated.** The count shown is what the engine
reported for the returned text, after the cap was applied.

**`llm` vs `extractive`.** `llm` is the shipping path: the model writes prose from
the retrieved chunks. `extractive` is the fallback used when Groq is unreachable or the
daily quota is spent — still grounded and cited, but assembled from the chunk text
rather than written. Both are shipping paths; only one is the normal one.

**Refusals still cite.** A declined question is shown a citation to the general
education page rather than to a scheme page, because no scheme page supports an
answer to it.

**`stage` and `reason`.** `stage` is where the answer ended up (`answered`,
`refused`, `ungrounded`); `reason` is the guardrail that fired, where one did.
